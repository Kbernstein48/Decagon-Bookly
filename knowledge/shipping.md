# Shipping policy

## Delivery speeds

Standard shipping is free on orders of $35 or more and normally arrives in 4-7 business days. Express shipping normally arrives in 2-3 business days. Next-day delivery is offered for eligible in-stock books ordered before 1:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday.

## Tracking and delays

A tracking link is emailed when an order leaves the warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first carrier scan. If tracking has not changed for 5 calendar days, Bookly support can open a carrier investigation. Weather, carrier disruptions, and remote delivery addresses can extend estimates.

## Shipping investigations

Bookly can open a simulated carrier investigation when tracking has not changed for 5 calendar days, an estimated delivery date has passed, a package was marked delivered but cannot be found after the customer checks household members, neighbors, and safe delivery locations, or a delivered package arrived damaged. Support must verify the order, record the reported issue, summarize the available carrier scans, and receive the customer's explicit confirmation before opening a case.

An open investigation receives a Bookly case ID and simulated carrier reference. The carrier's initial response is normally expected within 2 business days, although a final lost-package decision may take longer. Opening a case does not itself issue a replacement or refund.

## Address changes

The shipping address can be changed only while an order is still processing. Once an order has shipped, Bookly cannot redirect it. The customer may contact the carrier, but redirect availability is not guaranteed.

## Lost or damaged deliveries

Customers should report a package marked delivered but not received after checking household members, neighbors, and safe delivery locations. Bookly can replace or refund a confirmed lost shipment. Damaged books should be reported within 14 days of delivery; Bookly provides a prepaid label or replacement at no cost.
