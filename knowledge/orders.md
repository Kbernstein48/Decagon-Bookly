# Order policy

## Processing and cancellation

Orders can be cancelled or edited only while their status is processing. Warehouse allocation can happen quickly, so a cancellation is not guaranteed. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled and must use the return process after delivery.

## Identity verification

Support must use Bookly's secure account sign-in before revealing order status, titles, tracking details, or refund eligibility. Support should never ask for or disclose the email address stored on an order inside the conversation.

## Cart and checkout

Customers can ask Bookly Concierge to add, remove, or change the quantity of an exact book format in their browser-session cart. Prices, formats, and inventory must come from the current catalog. Clearing a full cart requires explicit confirmation. The concierge may prepare a checkout review, but it cannot submit payment or place the order; the customer must review and complete checkout personally.

## Shipping-address updates

A complete shipping address may be changed only while the order status is processing. Bookly must show the proposed address and receive explicit confirmation before applying it. Once warehouse fulfillment begins or the order ships, the address cannot be changed through support.

## Wishlists and availability alerts

Signed-in customers can save or remove catalog books from their wishlist. Back-in-stock alerts apply to one exact out-of-stock format and use the account email as the simulated notification channel. An alert is unnecessary when that format is currently available.

## Split shipments

An order may ship in more than one package when books leave different warehouses. Each shipment receives its own tracking number and estimate. The order is complete only after all fulfilled lines have been delivered.

## Preorders

Preorder dates are estimates supplied by publishers and may change. Bookly charges a payment method when the preorder prepares to ship. Customers can cancel a preorder before warehouse processing begins.
