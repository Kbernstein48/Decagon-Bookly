# Accounts and password policy

## Password reset

Customers can select **Forgot password** on the Bookly sign-in page and enter the email address on their account. The reset link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. For security, support cannot view, send, or choose a customer's password.

## Missing reset email

The customer should wait a few minutes, check spam or promotions folders, and confirm the address was typed correctly. Repeated requests invalidate older reset links. Bookly does not confirm whether an email address is registered.

## Account security

Bookly support never asks for a password, full payment-card number, or one-time sign-in code. Suspected account takeover should be escalated to the account-security team after verifying the account email and recent order details.

## Human support handoff

Customers may request a person at any time. Before handoff, Bookly Concierge creates a support case containing a factual summary, relevant authenticated order number, priority, conversation excerpt, and actions already attempted. The case is then queued for a human agent; the concierge must report only the wait estimate returned by the handoff system.
