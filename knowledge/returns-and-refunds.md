# Returns and refunds policy

## Eligibility

New, unread physical books may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The item must be in its original condition. Ebooks, gift cards, personalized items, and final-sale titles are not returnable unless defective or supplied in error.

## Refund calculation

Approved refunds include the returned item price and the tax allocated to that item. Original shipping charges are not refunded. Return-label fees are waived for damaged, defective, or incorrect items; otherwise a $4.95 label fee may be deducted from a mailed return.

## Timing

Bookly issues a refund after the return is approved in the support flow for this demo. Banks generally post card refunds within 5-10 business days. A refund confirmation includes a reference number, refunded titles, quantities, and amount.

## Partial returns

Customers may return selected order lines or part of the quantity on a multi-copy line. A line cannot be refunded for more units than were purchased, and a previously refunded unit cannot be refunded again.

## Return labels and status

Eligible physical books can receive a prepaid printable label or carrier QR code after the customer confirms the exact titles and quantities. A return ID identifies the request and can be used to check whether the label was created, the package is in transit, or the return was received. Creating a label does not itself issue a refund.

## Replacement shipments

Bookly can prepare a no-charge replacement for eligible delivered books that were lost, damaged, defective, or supplied incorrectly. Support must identify exact order lines and quantities, record the reason, summarize the proposed replacement, and receive explicit confirmation. An active replacement cannot be created twice for the same order.
